Hi, I'm Abhigith Neil Abraham.

I'm the founder and CEO of Vitalops. I spent 6 years in AI research and ML engineering; the papers are on Google Scholar. I've also led and contributed to popular open-source machine learning tools, on GitHub.

The work I keep for myself is inference: making LLMs serve far more tokens per second in production, then proving with evals that the accuracy survived.
